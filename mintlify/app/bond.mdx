---
title: "The bond page"
description: "The five tabs of a bond: order book, eligibility, coupons, collateral and risk."
---

Open a bond from the desk, or go to `/bonds/1`, `/bonds/2` or `/bonds/3`. The header shows the symbol, the
status, the terms in one line, and the best bid, best ask and mark. Below it: the token and settlement addresses
with copy buttons, the issuer, the next coupon, the current yield, the coverage and a HashScan link.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Order book">
    The ladder: asks from the highest down to the best ask, a strip with the spread and the mid, then bids from
    the best down. Each row shows the price, the size left, the cumulative size from the best price to that row (also drawn as a
    bar behind the row), the total in USDC, the maker and the expiry. Your
    own rows are marked and have a **Cancel** button.

    **Buy** on an ask or **Sell** on a bid opens the fill form under that row: an amount up to what the order
    has left, the total, a note on the fee, and the button. If your USDC or bond allowance to the market is too
    low, the app asks for the approval first.

    **Place an order** on the right: bid or ask, amount, price (the mark and the face are shown for reference),
    and an expiry. **Recent fills** lists trades with maker, taker and the HashScan link.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Eligibility">
    Your standing with this bond's token: KYC granted or not, frozen or not, Hedera account or not, bond status,
    and your bond balance. Two sentences give the token's live answer to a transfer from the issuer to you and
    from you to the issuer, with the EIP-1066 code.

    The **testnet KYC desk** panel requests or revokes KYC for the connected wallet.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Coupons">
    Every paid coupon with its snapshot id, amount and time, a **Claim** button for your share, and the pending
    schedule with its due time and HSS entity. The issuer also gets **Fund the pool**, **Pay coupon**,
    **Schedule** and, after maturity, **Redeem**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Collateral">
    The vault balance in HBAR, the live Chainlink HBAR/USD price, the coverage and the floor. The issuer gets
    **Deposit** and **Withdraw**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Risk">
    The registry status, the coverage, the mark and chain time on the left; the last verdict with its nonce,
    the coverage it observed, when it was issued, who relayed it, the trusted signer and the freshness window on
    the right. Below, the **verdict history** from `VerdictApplied` events, newest first.

    **Relayer**: paste a `VERDICT_JSON` line from a workflow run. The app parses it, verifies the EIP-712
    signature against the gate's trusted signer, shows the recovered address, and lets any wallet submit it.
    **Admin**: the desk admin sees **Unfreeze** when the bond is Frozen.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
