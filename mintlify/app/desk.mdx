## The front page

`/` is a single page for someone who has never seen the project: one statement, a live strip read from the
API (number of bonds, best ask, coverage, the last verdict with its nonce, the next coupon, the current block),
two hand-drawn diagrams of how a fill is judged and how a freeze happens, receipts with HashScan links, and the
three doors: the app, the API and the agents.

## The desk

`/desk` is the trading view. The app is built like a terminal: dark, dense, one screen per job, and driven from
the keyboard as much as the mouse.

### Bonds

One row per bond: status, coupon and interval, maturity, best bid and ask with the size offered at each, spread,
mark, current yield, coverage with its band (**thin** when it is low), depth (count of bids and asks), the last
fill and the next coupon. Every column sorts; click a row, or move with `j` and `k` and press `Enter`, to open
the bond.

### Your eligibility

Once a wallet is connected, this panel shows its Hedera account id, HBAR balance and HTS token count, and one
line per bond saying whether the bond token would accept a transfer to this wallet and why not.

### Testnet self-service

Three things a new wallet needs, in order:

1. **HBAR for gas**, from the [Hedera testnet faucet](https://portal.hedera.com/faucet). The panel shows your
   balance.
2. **Test USDC**: the settlement token is a mock USDC with an open mint. The button mints 10,000 to you.
3. **KYC on the bond tokens**: sign one message; the API's officer bot grants KYC on every bond token. See
   [compliance](/how-it-works/compliance).

## The header and the live strip

The top bar carries the pages and the wallet chip with the connected address and its HBAR balance. Clicking the
chip opens the wallet chooser or disconnects. If the wallet is on another network, a bar asks it to switch to
Hedera testnet and shows the network details for adding it by hand.

Under it, one line of live numbers on every page: the HBAR/USD feed, the latest block, each bond's best bid, best
ask and coverage (click one to open the bond), and the last verdict with its nonce and age.

## Keyboard

Press `?` on any page for the list. Shortcuts pause while a field has focus.

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| `d` `c` `a` | Desk, Compliance, Activity |
| `1` to `9` | open bond n |
| `j` `k` `Enter` | move through the desk grid and open the row |
| `[` `]` | previous and next tab on a bond |
| `b` `s` | buy or sell in the order form |
| `↑` `↓` | nudge the order price by 0.005 |
| `Enter` | place the order, or fill the open row |
| `Esc` | cancel a confirm, close the fill form |

## Every write, the same way

Every transaction the app sends goes through one pipeline: a dry run first, so a revert is decoded into a
sentence before anything is signed; then send, wait for the receipt, and refresh. Confirm buttons arm for eight
seconds and disarm on their own.
