## The front page

`/` is a single page for someone who has never seen the project: one statement, a live strip read from the
API (number of bonds, best ask, coverage, the last verdict with its nonce, the next coupon, the current block),
two hand-drawn diagrams of how a fill is judged and how a freeze happens, receipts with HashScan links, and the
three doors: the app, the API and the agents.

## The desk

### Bonds

One row per bond: status, coupon, maturity, best bid and ask, depth (count of bids and asks), current yield,
coverage with a **thin** tag when it is low, the next coupon, and a HashScan link. Click the symbol to open the
bond. The yield column sorts.

### Your eligibility

Once a wallet is connected, this panel shows its Hedera account id, HBAR balance and HTS token count, and one
line per bond saying whether the bond token would accept a transfer to this wallet and why not.

### Testnet self-service

Three things a new wallet needs, in order:

1. **HBAR for gas**, from the [Hedera testnet faucet](https://portal.hedera.com/faucet). The panel shows your
   balance.
2. **Test USDC**: the settlement token is a mock USDC with an open mint. The button mints 10,000 to you.
3. **KYC on the bond tokens**: sign one message; the API's officer bot grants KYC on every bond token. See
   [compliance](/how-it-works/compliance).

## The header

The top bar shows the live HBAR/USD price and the latest block, and the wallet chip with the connected address
and its HBAR balance. Clicking the chip opens the wallet chooser or disconnects. If the wallet is on another
network, a bar asks it to switch to Hedera testnet and shows the network details for adding it by hand.

## Every write, the same way

Every transaction the app sends goes through one pipeline: a dry run first, so a revert is decoded into a
sentence before anything is signed; then send, wait for the receipt, and refresh. Confirm buttons arm for eight
seconds and disarm on their own.
