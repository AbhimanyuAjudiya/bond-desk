## Chainlink CRE workflows

Both run on the CRE network from the private registry since 2026-09-12.

| Workflow | Runs | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `bond-monitor-production` | every hour | Reads bond 1's coverage inside the enclave, signs a verdict, delivers it to RiskGate itself |
| `liquidation-protection-production` | every 30 seconds | Defends a position in Chainlink's liquidation challenge on Sepolia |

The monitor watches bond 1 only. Bonds 2 and 3 have books and coupons but no monitor; their status changes by
hand or through the simulator.
