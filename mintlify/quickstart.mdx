---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Buy a test bond on Hedera testnet in about five minutes, with MetaMask and nothing else."
---

You need a browser with MetaMask and about five minutes. Everything you touch is a test asset.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Hedera testnet to MetaMask">
    Open the desk at [wd6nrvmajt.ap-south-1.awsapprunner.com/desk](https://wd6nrvmajt.ap-south-1.awsapprunner.com/desk)
    and click **Connect**. Pick MetaMask by name. The app asks MetaMask to add and switch to Hedera testnet for
    you. If you prefer to add it by hand, use these values:

    | Field | Value |
    |---|---|
    | Network name | Hedera testnet |
    | RPC URL | `https://testnet.hashio.io/api` |
    | Chain id | `296` |
    | Currency symbol | `HBAR` |
    | Block explorer | `https://hashscan.io/testnet` |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get test HBAR for gas">
    Every transaction is paid in HBAR. Copy your address from the wallet chip at the top right and paste it into
    the [Hedera testnet faucet](https://portal.hedera.com/faucet). The desk shows your balance under
    **Testnet self-service** once the HBAR arrives.

    <Note>
      An address that has never received HBAR does not exist as a Hedera account yet. Fund it before anything
      else; the **Eligibility** tab says "no Hedera account" until you do.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get test USDC">
    On the desk, click **Get 10,000 test USDC**. The settlement token is a mock USDC with an open mint, so
    anyone can take some. Confirm in MetaMask.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request testnet KYC">
    Click **Request testnet KYC**. MetaMask asks you to sign a one-line message; no gas is spent. The API's
    compliance-officer bot then grants KYC to your address on every bond token. On testnet the officer approves
    anyone who asks. In production it is a human or a KYC provider. Either way the token, not the page, enforces
    the result.

    The **Eligibility** tab of any bond turns from **not eligible** to **KYC granted** once the grant lands,
    usually within fifteen seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy from the order book">
    Open a bond from the desk (BDB28 is a good first one), stay on the **Order book** tab and click **Buy** on
    one of the red ask rows. Enter an amount up to the size shown, then confirm. MetaMask asks twice: once to
    approve USDC to the market, once for the fill itself.

    The trade appears under **Recent fills** with its HashScan link, the ask shrinks, and your bond balance
    shows on the **Eligibility** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See what the token would have said">
    Curious what happens without KYC? Revoke your own KYC from the **Eligibility** tab, then try the same
    **Buy**. The app runs the fill as a dry run first and shows the token's answer,
    `ComplianceRejected(0x10, InvalidKycStatus)`, before anything is signed. Nothing is sent; nothing moves.
    Request KYC again to continue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you just did

- The bond you bought is an ATS security token. Its own compliance check ran inside the fill.
- The price you paid is in USDC per bond, quoted against a live mark from the Chainlink HBAR/USD feed.
- Your holding earns the next coupon. When it is paid, the **Coupons** tab shows a **Claim** button.

## If something refuses

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `ComplianceRejected(0x10, InvalidKycStatus)` | The token has not granted KYC to your address | Request testnet KYC on the Eligibility tab |
| `BondNotActive(1, Frozen)` | The risk gate has frozen this bond | Wait for the admin to unfreeze, or trade another bond |
| "no Hedera account" | The address has never received HBAR | Use the faucet |
| `BadAmount` | You asked for more than the order has left | Enter an amount up to the size shown |

The [errors reference](/reference/errors) lists every message the app can decode.
