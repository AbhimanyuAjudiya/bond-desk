title: "Coupons and maturity"
description: "How the BondLifecycle contract pays coupons, how the Hedera Schedule Service fires them on time, and how holders claim and redeem."
---

Coupons are handled by the contract `BondLifecycle`. It holds a USDC pool per bond, pays each coupon against a
snapshot of holders, and asks the Hedera Schedule Service (HSS) to call it again when the next coupon is due.

## The pool

The issuer funds coupons in advance with `fund(bondId, amount)`, which pulls USDC into the pool. `couponDue`
shows what the next coupon would cost against the live supply. A coupon that is due but not funded reverts with
`CouponUnderfunded(due, funded)`, and the app says so before you sign.

## Paying a coupon

`payCoupon(bondId)` can be called by anyone once the coupon is due. It is what the scheduled execution calls.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Checks">
    The bond must be Active or Frozen, the due second must have passed, and there must be a coupon left before
    maturity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Snapshot">
    The contract takes an ATS snapshot of every holder's balance. The coupon is computed on the supply at that
    snapshot: face value, times the annual rate, times the interval, for every bond.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reserve and record">
    The amount is moved from the pool to the coupon record. `nextCoupon` advances by one interval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule the next one">
    From inside the same call, the contract asks HSS to run `payCoupon` again at the next due second. See below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Holders then pull their share with `claim(bondId, couponId)`: their balance at the snapshot, divided by the
snapshot supply, times the coupon. `claimable` shows the unclaimed amount. A holder who joined after the snapshot
gets nothing for that coupon.

## How the network fires it

Hedera's Schedule Service is a system contract at address `0x16b`. Since HIP-1215, a contract can ask it to
call any function at a chosen future second with `scheduleCall`. Bond Desk uses it like this:

| Detail | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Gas reserved per execution | 4,000,000 | The call takes a snapshot on the ATS token and schedules the next run; the first attempt with 2,000,000 ran out of gas |
| Capacity probing | up to 8 probes | A second can be full; the contract probes later seconds with a back-off until one has capacity |
| Lag | 10 seconds after the due second | A scheduled execution runs in a block whose timestamp can sit a couple of seconds before the target second. Coupon 2 fired two seconds early and reverted `CouponNotDue`; the lag fixes that |
| Payer | the lifecycle contract itself | The contract that schedules a call pays for its execution, so it keeps a small HBAR float |
| Re-arm | permissionless `schedule(bondId)` | If a scheduled run fails to arm (network busy, nested-scheduling limit), anyone can re-arm it |

The proof that a coupon was paid lives on the mirror node, not in the scheduling receipt. A schedule that
"executed" may have reverted inside; the check that matters is `executed_timestamp` on the schedule plus the
child transaction's result. The harness script `harness/scripts/validate-schedule.sh` checks both.

<Note>
  Coupon 3 of bond 1 is the clean example: HSS schedule
  [0.0.10482928](https://hashscan.io/testnet/schedule/0.0.10482928) executed at its second, the child call
  succeeded, coupon id 2 was recorded and the next schedule
  [0.0.10498485](https://hashscan.io/testnet/schedule/0.0.10498485) was armed from inside that call. Bonds 2 and
  3 have their first coupons armed the same way.
</Note>

## Maturity

After the maturity date, `redeem(bondId)` pays the caller's principal from the pool (balance times face value),
burns their bonds through the token's maturity redeemer role, and moves the bond to Matured on the first
redemption. A defaulted bond cannot be redeemed; its holders claim seized collateral instead
([collateral](/how-it-works/collateral)).

## In the app

The **Coupons** tab lists every paid coupon with its snapshot, the pending schedule with its due time, and a
**Claim** button per coupon for the connected wallet. The issuer sees **Fund**, **Pay coupon**, **Schedule** and
**Redeem** there too.
