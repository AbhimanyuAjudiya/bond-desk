The order book is the contract `BondMarket`. Every order is a row in its storage; there is no off-chain matching
engine. The app and the API rebuild the ladder from the contract and show trades from the mirror node.

## Placing an order

`place(bondId, isSell, amount, price, expiry)` writes an order and emits `OrderPlaced`.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `isSell` | `true` for an ask, `false` for a bid |
| `amount` | Whole bonds; the bond tokens have zero decimals |
| `price` | USDC per bond in base units: `990000` is 0.99 USDC |
| `expiry` | A unix second, or `0` for good-till-cancelled. The app uses seven days |

Nothing is locked when an order is placed. Allowances are only used at fill time: a bid needs USDC approved to
the market, an ask needs bonds approved to the market. The app asks for the approval right before the fill.

A price band can be switched on per bond (`setBand`), which rejects orders too far from the oracle mark with
`PriceOutOfBand`. It is off for all three demo bonds.

`cancel(orderId)` removes an order; only its maker can call it.

## Filling an order

`fill(orderId, amount)` runs these checks in this order and reverts on the first failure:

<Steps>
  <Step title="The order exists and has not expired">
    `OrderNotFound` if it was filled or cancelled, `OrderExpired` if its expiry has passed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The amount is sane">
    `BadAmount` if it is zero, larger than what the order has left, or rounds to nothing in USDC.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The bond is Active">
    `BondNotActive(bondId, status)` otherwise. A frozen bond fails here, whoever the buyer is.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The token allows the transfer">
    The market calls the bond token's `canTransferFrom(seller, buyer, amount, "")`. Anything but allowed reverts
    with `ComplianceRejected(code, reason)`. See [the bond token](/how-it-works/bond-token) for the order of
    the token's own checks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settlement">
    USDC moves from the buyer to the seller, plus the fee to the treasury. Then the bonds move from the seller
    to the buyer with the token's `transferFrom`. Both happen in the same transaction, so a failure anywhere
    leaves both sides untouched.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A fill can be partial. The order keeps the remainder; a full fill deletes the row.

## Cost and fee

The cost of taking `amount` bonds at `price` is `amount × price` in USDC base units (the bond has zero
decimals). The fee is a percentage of that cost, charged to the buyer on top and sent to the treasury. It is
currently **0%** and can never exceed 1%. The maker pays nothing.

## What the book shows

- **Best bid, best ask, spread, mid** come from the open, unexpired orders. `quote(bondId)` on the contract
  returns the best bid and ask.
- **Depth** on the desk is the count of bids and asks.
- **Recent fills** come from `Filled` events read through the mirror node, so a new trade appears a few seconds
  after it lands.
- **Your rows** are marked and can be cancelled from the ladder.

## Reading the numbers on the API

`GET /bonds/2/orderbook` returns bids sorted by price descending, asks ascending, and the last fifty fills.
Prices and amounts are decimal strings in base units. See the [API reference](/api/overview).
