## The vault

`CollateralVault` holds HBAR per bond. Only the issuer can deposit and withdraw.

| Call | Who | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `deposit(bondId)` with value | issuer | While the bond is Active or Frozen |
| `withdraw(bondId, amount)` | issuer | While Active or Matured, never after a seizure, and only if coverage stays at or above the floor (`CoverageTooLow` otherwise) |
| `seize(bondId)` | the risk gate | On a DEFAULT verdict: snapshots the holders and locks the whole balance for pro-rata claims |
| `claimSeized(bondId)` | any holder at the seizure snapshot | Their share, once |

The floor (`minCoverageBps`) is a desk-wide setting the admin can change. On Hedera the EVM counts value in
tinybar: sending 100 HBAR through the JSON-RPC relay stores `10,000,000,000` in the contract.

## Coverage

Coverage answers "how much of the outstanding principal does the collateral cover right now", in basis points:

```text
coverageBps = (collateral in HBAR × HBAR/USD price) / (bonds outstanding × face value) × 10,000
```

The price comes from Chainlink's HBAR/USD aggregator on Hedera testnet
([0x59bC…B4a](https://hashscan.io/testnet/contract/0x59bC155EB6c6C415fE43255aF66EcF0523c92B4a), 8 decimals),
read on chain by `NavOracle.hbarUsd()`. If the feed has not updated within the allowed age the read reverts
`StaleFeed`, and the risk gate's snapshot reports `feedFresh: false` instead of a number, so a stale price can
never look like a healthy one.

Bond 1 was backed with 100 HBAR against 100 bonds, which gave a coverage of 762 bps at the time. Faucet-sized
collateral keeps every demo number small; a real bond would sit far above 100%. The ladder the enclave applies
is the same, only the numbers differ.

## The mark

`NavOracle.mark(bondId)` is the reference price the desk shows next to the bid and ask: the face value plus
the coupon accrued so far in the current period, and the face value after maturity. It never reads the price
feed, so it is always available. The order book can be told to reject orders too far from the mark with a
per-bond band; the demo bonds have the band switched off.

## In the app

The **Collateral** tab shows the vault balance, the live feed price and the coverage, and gives the issuer the
**Deposit** and **Withdraw** forms. A withdrawal that would drop coverage below the floor is refused before it
is signed.

<Note>
  The Hedera relay under-estimates gas for a contract call that forwards HBAR, so `withdraw` is sent with an
  explicit gas limit of 400,000. The app sets it for you; from a script, pass `--gas-limit 400000`.
</Note>
