---
title: "If you are an investor"
description: "Get set up, buy and sell bonds, claim coupons, redeem at maturity."
---

You need MetaMask on Hedera testnet, test HBAR and test USDC, and KYC on the bond tokens. The
[quickstart](/quickstart) walks through all four in five minutes.

## Buy

1. Open a bond from the desk. Read the header: best bid, best ask, mark, coverage, next coupon.
2. On the **Order book** tab, click **Buy** on an ask. Enter up to the amount shown. Confirm the USDC approval
   if asked, then the fill.
3. The trade shows under **Recent fills**; your balance shows on the **Eligibility** tab.

If you would rather wait for a price, place a **bid** with **Place an order**: amount, price in USDC per bond,
expiry. Nothing is locked until someone fills it. You can cancel it from the ladder at any time.

## Sell

Click **Sell** on a bid, or place an **ask**. A fill needs your bonds approved to the market; the app asks
for the approval first.

## Coupons

When a coupon is paid, the **Coupons** tab shows it with a **Claim** button. Your share is your balance at the
coupon's snapshot divided by the supply at that snapshot. Claim any time; nothing expires.

## Redeem

After the maturity date, **Redeem** on the Coupons tab pays your principal in USDC and burns your bonds.

## What can stop you

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ComplianceRejected(0x10, InvalidKycStatus)` | You, or the counterparty, have no KYC on this token |
| `ComplianceRejected(0x10, AccountIsBlocked)` | One side is frozen by the officer |
| `BondNotActive(id, Frozen)` | The risk gate has frozen the bond; wait for the admin to unfreeze |
| `OrderExpired`, `OrderNotFound` | The order left the book; refresh the ladder |

<Tip>
  The app runs every fill as a dry run first and shows the reason before you sign, so a refusal costs no gas.
</Tip>
