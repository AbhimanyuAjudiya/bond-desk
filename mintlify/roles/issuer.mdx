---
title: "If you are the issuer"
description: "Issue a bond, post inventory, fund coupons, back it with collateral, and keep it trading."
---

The issuer is the wallet that created the bond through the ATS factory. It holds the unsold units, funds the
coupon pool, posts collateral and, as the desk admin, can unfreeze.

## Issue

Issuance is a script, not a page: `ats/script/CreateBond.s.sol` calls the live ATS factory, grants KYC in the
order ATS requires, mints the supply to you and appends the token to `ats/testnet.json`. The desk contracts
then `register` the bond with its terms. See [run the contracts](/run/contracts).

## Sell inventory

On the bond's **Order book** tab, **Place an order** as an ask: amount, price, expiry. Approve the bond token
to the market when the first fill needs it. Every fill runs the token's compliance check on the buyer, so a
non-KYC wallet can never take your ask.

## Fund coupons

On the **Coupons** tab, **Fund the pool** with USDC before a coupon is due. The tab shows what the next coupon
costs. A scheduled coupon that finds an underfunded pool reverts and has to be paid by hand once funded.

The Hedera Schedule Service pays each coupon on time and arms the next one from inside the executed call. If a
schedule fails to arm, **Schedule** re-arms it; anyone may call it.

## Post collateral

On the **Collateral** tab, **Deposit** HBAR. Coverage updates immediately from the Chainlink price.
**Withdraw** works while coverage stays above the floor. Remember that the enclave monitor reads coverage every
hour: a withdrawal that drops coverage below its private FREEZE line halts trading at the next run.

## When the bond is frozen

The **Risk** tab shows the verdict that froze it and the coverage it observed. Restore collateral, then
**Unfreeze** from the Admin panel. The next hourly verdict decides again.

## At maturity

Pay any coupon still due, then holders **Redeem** against the pool. Fund the pool with the principal first.
